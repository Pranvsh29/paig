Chapter IOrigin

It begins at 2,150 metres.

Our Gedeb Halo grows on the ridges above Yirgacheffe, where cool nights slow the cherry down and concentrate its sugars. A Halvard buyer visits during every harvest. We agree the price before picking starts, and it sits well above the commodity market.
