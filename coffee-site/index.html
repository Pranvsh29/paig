From cherry to cup

Five checkpoints. No batch skips one.

A coffee reaches you only after it has passed every stage below. Anything that misses a spec is sold as green coffee or goes to our staff café. It never reaches the shelf.

  1. 01 / SOURCE

    Buy at harvest

    Our buyers visit each partner during harvest. Contracts fix the price before picking starts, and the price sits above the commodity market.

    Avg. premium: +68% over C-price
  2. 02 / GRADE

    Grade the green

    We pull a 350 g sample from each lot, count the defects and measure moisture and density before a single bag is shipped.

    Moisture 10.0–12.0% · ≤5 defects
  3. 03 / PROFILE

    Build the profile

    Each new lot gets sample-roasted at three development times and cupped blind. The winning curve becomes the production target.

    100 g sample roasts · 3 levels
  4. 04 / ROAST

    Roast to the curve

    Production roasts are logged once a second against the target curve. Operators adjust gas and airflow by hand to stay on it.

    Tolerance: ±2 Agtron of target
  5. 05 / CUP

    Cup every batch

    Within 24 hours of roasting, the quality team cups a sample from every batch. Two signatures release it for packing.

    SCA protocol · 3 cuppers per table
